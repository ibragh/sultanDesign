Five directions for the apartment website

Three concepts follow the landing-page-design skill to the letter. Two are original ideas built around how guests in Saudi Arabia actually book: Arabic first with WhatsApp, and an app style finder for every unit. Each one is a working page, so click around.

Placeholders. The brand name “Liwan”, the eight apartments, prices and reviews are stand‑ins, and the photos are stock images until the owner’s own photos arrive. Every photo sits in one folder, so swapping them in changes all five concepts at once.

The second question

Can the site connect to Airbnb and Gathern?

Yes. There are three levels.

Neither platform lets an outside website take bookings for it. What the site can do is link to the listings and share one availability calendar with both platforms. That prevents double bookings while the site sells the same apartments direct, without the platform fee.

Level 1 · link out

Buttons to each listing

Every apartment page gets “View on Airbnb” and “View on Gathern” buttons. Gathern gives each host a shareable link to their property page.

Effort
An afternoon
Cost
Free
Syncs
Nothing
Risk
None, but no direct bookings either
Level 2 · calendar sync

Shared calendar over iCal

Airbnb can export each listing’s calendar as a link and import other calendars. The site’s booking engine imports the Airbnb and Gathern calendars and sends its own bookings back.

Effort
A booking plugin or small backend
Cost
Free to low
Syncs
Booked and blocked dates only, not prices or messages
Risk
Airbnb refreshes imported calendars every 3 hours, so a busy night can be sold twice
Level 3 · recommended for many units

Channel manager

Software such as Hostaway, Guesty or Hospitable is an official Airbnb partner. It syncs availability, prices and messages in near real time, and most include a direct booking website that can use one of these designs.

Effort
Setup once, then less daily work
Cost
Monthly fee per unit
Syncs
Airbnb fully. Gathern through iCal
Risk
Lowest

What to check before choosing

Gathern. Gathern’s own pages don’t mention calendar sync. Saudi tools such as Naseeq list Gathern next to Airbnb in their iCal sync, so it looks possible. Every Gathern host has a dedicated account manager, so ask them directly: “Can I import and export my calendar as an iCal (.ics) link?” None of the big international channel managers we checked list Gathern as a connected channel.

Airbnb fees. Airbnb now charges most hosts a single fee of about 15.5% of each booking, and moved hosts who use connected software onto it first. Every direct booking keeps that margin. Some of it can go to guests as a lower direct price, which is the case concepts 2 and 5 make. Their comparison totals are examples.

Also needed for direct bookings in Saudi Arabia

  • A Ministry of Tourism license for each unit, with the number shown on its page. Airbnb already asks for it.
  • A local payment gateway, such as Moyasar, Tap or HyperPay, to accept mada, Apple Pay and STC Pay. Tabby or Tamara can add split payments.
  • WhatsApp Business for confirmations and door codes. Guests here expect it.
  • Guest ID collection at booking, as the ministry requires.
