design, not content.

## Booking and policy facts

- Check in from 3 pm, check out by 12 pm. Door code arrives on WhatsApp at 1 pm on arrival day.
- Free cancellation until 72 hours before check in. After that, the first night is non refundable.
- Payment: mada, Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, STC Pay. Tabby: split into 4 payments.
- Long stays: 10% off 7+ nights, 25% off 28+ nights. Weekly housekeeping on stays over 7 nights.
- ID: national ID, Iqama or passport for each adult guest, as the Ministry of Tourism requires.
- Early check in or late check out: free when the calendar allows, ask on WhatsApp.
- Every unit holds a Ministry of Tourism license (numbers above). Show them.

## Channels (important for the UX story)

The same apartments are listed on Airbnb and Gathern. The website should:
1. Push **book direct** as the primary path (best price, no service fee, same apartment, same calendar).
2. Still offer "Also on Airbnb" and "Also on Gathern" as trust signals and alternatives
   (link to `https://www.airbnb.com/` and `https://gathern.co/en` for now; real listing links come later).
3. Show availability as one calendar "synced with Airbnb and Gathern" (example sync status: "Calendar synced 6 min ago").
   Example price of the same 3 weeknight stay at Malqa Loft: Direct SAR 1,262 (3 x 389 + 95 cleaning). On a
   platform the guest typically pays more because of service fees; when you show a comparison, label it
   "Example" and keep platform totals plausible (Airbnb SAR 1,431, Gathern SAR 1,389).

## Technical contract (every design)

- Output: ONE standalone file `/home/user/sultanDesign/designs/<slug>.html`. It is published as a supporting file
  of a multi page artifact, so it gets NO skeleton: start with `<!doctype html>`, `<html lang=…>`,
  `<meta charset="utf-8">`, `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, viewport-fit=cover">`,
  a `<title>`, a meta description, then your `<style>`. Give `body` an explicit background and set `margin:0`.
- Fonts: Google Fonts `<link>` only, always with a real fallback stack.
- Scripts: vanilla inline JS. No libraries unless essential (allowed hosts only: cdnjs, jsdelivr/npm, unpkg, pinned versions).
- No external images, no iframes (maps must be drawn in SVG/CSS), no `alert/confirm/prompt`, no `window.print`.
  Forms: handle `submit` with `preventDefault()` and show the result inline.
- External links (`wa.me`, airbnb.com, gathern.co, socials): `target="_blank" rel="noopener"`. Show the WhatsApp
  number as text too.
- Icons: Phosphor, inline. Put the literal marker `<!--ICONS-->` right after `<body>`, reference icons as
  `<svg class="i" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#i-NAME"/></svg>` (style `.i{width:1em;height:1em}` or sized via CSS),
  then run `python3 /home/user/sultanDesign/shared/inline-icons.py designs/<slug>.html` (idempotent, rerun after edits).
  Available names: see `shared/icons-sprite.svg.html` (e.g. bed bathtub users users-three wifi-high car coffee
  key lock-key door map-pin calendar-blank calendar-check star star-fill heart heart-fill whatsapp-logo
  whatsapp-logo-fill phone chat-circle-dots shield-check check check-circle x list magnifying-glass
  sliders-horizontal arrow-right arrow-left arrow-up-right caret-down caret-left caret-right plus minus globe
  translate buildings house-line sun moon clock broom desktop snowflake baby mosque airplane-tilt train
  storefront info warning-circle seal-check instagram-logo tiktok-logo x-logo snapchat-logo compass fork-knife
  swimming-pool barbell elevator tag receipt credit-card apple-logo quotes images camera frame-corners
  arrows-out funnel ruler stairs plant hand-coins percent lightning car-profile person-simple-walk eye
  squares-four list-bullets copy link clock-countdown coins hourglass television washing-machine cooking-pot).
  A RTL page must mirror directional arrows (`[dir=rtl] .i-dir{transform:scaleX(-1)}`).
- Layout: side gutter `padding-left: max(16px, env(safe-area-inset-left, 0px))` and the right equivalent on the
  inner wrapper of every full width band; vertical padding with `padding-block`. No horizontal page scroll at
  390px. `min-width:0` on grid/flex children with text. Images `max-width:100%`. Sticky headers use
  `top: env(safe-area-inset-top, 0px)`; fixed bottom bars add `env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 0px)`.
- Page complete at rest: nothing meant to be read may sit at `opacity:0` waiting for scroll. For scroll reveals
  use this pattern: on the first `scroll` event (passive, once), mark only elements that are still fully below
  the viewport as `.pre`, then reveal them with IntersectionObserver (once). A full page screenshot taken without
  scrolling must show every section.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → no movement, only opacity, or nothing.
- Visible focus rings on everything interactive; a skip link; semantic landmarks; `font-variant-numeric:
  tabular-nums` for prices and dates.
- Back link to the gallery: a small fixed pill, bottom left (respect safe-area insets), text "All concepts"
  with an arrow, `href="../index.html"`, styled to fit the design but clearly secondary. On mobile it must not
  cover the design's own sticky booking bar (stack above it or move it to the top).
- Single theme is fine (each concept commits to one look) but set every color explicitly via tokens on `:root`
  and set `color-scheme` to match.

### D4 `designs/04-najdi.html`: own concept, Arabic first, bilingual
Default `lang="ar" dir="rtl"`, with a working language toggle to English (`dir="ltr"`), all visible copy in both
languages (use a dictionary + `data-i18n` keys). Identity rooted in Najdi architecture: mud plaster walls,
triangular crenellation (shurfa) rooflines, and the geometric painted Najdi doors (teal, saffron, oxide red,
charcoal) used as small geometric bands and a door shaped hero frame, drawn in SVG/CSS, never as clipart.
Typography: a characterful Arabic display face (e.g. Reem Kufi or Aref Ruqaa) for headings with restraint, a clean
Arabic+Latin body face (e.g. IBM Plex Sans Arabic + IBM Plex Sans, or Readex Pro). WhatsApp first booking:
the primary CTA opens a short inline form (apartment, dates, guests, name) that composes a prefilled WhatsApp
message link and shows the message preview; secondary "Book online". Show dates in both Gregorian and Hijri
(use `Intl.DateTimeFormat('ar-SA-u-ca-islamic-umalqura')`). Family signals (private entrance, majlis), Qibla and
nearby mosque, Ministry of Tourism license on each card, Riyadh Season note. Apartment cards, reviews (Arabic
reviews translated), FAQ, "Also on Airbnb and Gathern", footer. Avoid the generic cream + serif + terracotta look:
the painted door palette on lime plaster is the point.

### D5 `designs/05-finder.html`: own concept, the booking app
A product UI rather than a landing page: an app like search for all 8 apartments. Top bar with brand, date range,
guests, and filters (bedrooms, max price slider, amenities chips: parking, workspace, private entrance, garden or
terrace, gym or pool). Split view: result list (cards with photo carousel dots, price for the chosen dates, rating,
"Free cancellation") and a stylised SVG map of north Riyadh (schematic roads: King Fahd Road, Northern Ring Road,
King Salman Road, Olaya Street; district labels; label it "Schematic map") with price pins. Hover/focus syncs card
and pin. Click opens a detail drawer: gallery, facts, a two month availability calendar (booked nights greyed,
legend: "Synced from Airbnb, Gathern and direct bookings"), price breakdown, and three ways to book with totals:
Direct (highlighted as lowest, primary), Airbnb, Gathern (example totals). Compare tray: tick up to 3 apartments,
open a side by side compare table. Sort (recommended, price, rating). Empty state when filters exclude everything,
with a "Clear filters" action. Mobile: list first with a "Map" toggle button; drawer becomes a full screen sheet.
Must be theme aware (light + dark tokens following the artifact contract: bare `:root` light, dark under
`prefers-color-scheme` guarded by `:root:not([data-theme="light"])`, and `:root[data-theme="dark"]`).
Typography: pick a distinctive but highly legible UI pairing (not Inter, not Space Grotesk), tabular numbers.
Accent: one confident hue that works on both themes.
