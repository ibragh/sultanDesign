Questions

What past guests ask before booking direct

Still unsure? Message the hosts before you book.

Ask the hosts on WhatsApp
Is it the same apartment I saw on Airbnb?

Yes. All seven homes are the same listings on Airbnb, with the same photos, the same hosts and the same 517 reviews. Each apartment on this page links to its Airbnb listing, so you can check before you book.

Why is it cheaper here?

Airbnb takes a fee on every booking. When you book with the hosts directly there is no platform in the middle, so you pay the nightly rate and the service fee is SAR 0.

When do I pay?

Only after the host confirms. Signing the contract holds your dates. Sultan or Hussam then confirm on WhatsApp and send the payment details. Nothing is charged on this page.

Do I still get a door code?

Yes. Every apartment has a smart lock. Your code arrives on WhatsApp before 4 pm on arrival day, and you can let yourself in any time after that.

What do you need from me?

Your full name as on your ID, your nationality, your ID, Iqama or passport number, a mobile number and the number of companions. They go into the stay contract and are not stored on this site.

Can I still book on Airbnb?

Yes. Every apartment stays listed on Airbnb, and both share one calendar, so a night booked there disappears here. Booking direct only skips the fee.
