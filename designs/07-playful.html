FAQ

Questions people ask before booking outside Airbnb

Still unsure about something? Ask the hosts on WhatsApp at +966 50 489 5569.

Is booking outside Airbnb safe?

You book with the same hosts who run the Airbnb listings, and you sign a contract that names the home, your dates, the price and the house rules. You pay nothing on this site, and nothing at all until the host confirms on WhatsApp.

What if the home gets booked on Airbnb at the same time?

Your dates are held as soon as you sign. Each home has one calendar synced with Airbnb, and the host checks it again before confirming. If a clash ever slipped through, you would not have paid anything.

When and how do I pay?

After the host confirms your dates on WhatsApp, they send the agreed amount and the payment details. This site never asks for card details.

What happens to my ID details?

They are used only to fill in your contract on your phone. The site does not store them. The contract travels in the WhatsApp message you send to the host.

How do I get in?

Every home has a smart lock. The door code comes on WhatsApp before you arrive, so you can let yourself in any time after 4:00 pm, even if you land late at night.

Can I cancel?

Cancellation terms are agreed with the host before you pay and written into your contract.

What are the check in and check out times?

Check in from 4:00 pm and check out by 12:00 pm. A late check out needs the host’s approval and may cost extra.

Can I smoke or bring a pet?

No smoking of any kind inside, including vapes and shisha, and no charcoal. Pets are welcome only with the host’s approval before you book.
