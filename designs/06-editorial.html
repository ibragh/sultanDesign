How do I get in?

Every apartment has a smart lock. Once the hosts confirm and you pay, your own door code arrives on WhatsApp. Check in is from 4:00 pm, at any hour after that, with no key handover.

Is it the same apartment as on Airbnb?

Yes. All seven apartments are listed on Airbnb by the same hosts, Sultan and Hussam. Each one links to its listing, so you can check the photos and reviews there first.

Why is booking direct cheaper?

Airbnb keeps a fee of around 15.5% of every booking. Here there is no platform in the middle, so the hosts can price lower and there is no service fee.

When do I pay?

Only after the hosts confirm your dates. Signing the contract holds the dates. The hosts then message you on WhatsApp with the total and how to pay.

What ID do you need?

A national ID, Iqama or passport number for the main guest, written into the stay contract. It is used only to create the contract and is not stored on this site.

What are the check in and check out times?

Check in from 4:00 pm and check out by 12:00 pm. A late check out depends on the next booking, so ask the hosts on WhatsApp.

Can I smoke inside?

No. Smoking of any kind is not allowed inside, including vapes and shisha, and charcoal is not allowed either.

Are pets allowed?

Only with the hosts' approval before you book. Ask on WhatsApp and say what pet it is.

Which apartment suits a family?

Prince Fawaz Home sleeps 6, with a kids room, a high chair and a baby cot. Al Ajawid House has three bedrooms for up to 6 guests.

How far is the airport?

About 15 minutes by car from Al Nahda and Madinah Road, and about 35 minutes from Al Ajawid and Prince Fawaz, depending on traffic.

Privacy

We collect only what the stay contract needs: your full name, nationality, ID, Iqama or passport number, mobile number, and the names of any companions you add.

These details are used to create the contract and to reach you on WhatsApp about your stay. This site does not store your ID details and sets no tracking cookies.
